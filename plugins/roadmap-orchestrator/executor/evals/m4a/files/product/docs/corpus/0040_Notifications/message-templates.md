# Message templates

The wording of the texts Tidewater sends. Keep them short: one text, no links.

## Booking confirmed

> Tidewater: {vessel} is booked into berth {berth} on {date} for the {hw} high water (window {opens}-{closes}).
> Ref {id}.

## Booking cancelled

> Tidewater: booking {id} for {vessel} in berth {berth} on {date} is cancelled. The berth is free again.

## Notes on wording

- Use the vessel's name as registered, not the skipper's.
- Dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`; we tried "Sat 4 Oct" and people misread it in a hurry.
- Times are harbour time. Do not add a time zone; nobody here needs one.
- Always end with the booking id, so a skipper can quote it.

## Changing a template

Ask the harbour master. Skippers learn the shape of these texts and notice when it changes, so change them rarely
and all at once.
