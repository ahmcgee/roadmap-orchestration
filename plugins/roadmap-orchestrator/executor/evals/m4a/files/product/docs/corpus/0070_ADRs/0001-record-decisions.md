# ADR 0001: Record design decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2024-11-02

## Context

Tidewater is maintained by whoever has time over the winter. Decisions made one winter were forgotten by the next,
and the same arguments came round again.

## Decision

We will record each significant design decision as a short numbered record in this folder: the context, the
decision and its consequences. Records are not edited after they are accepted; a later record supersedes an
earlier one and says so.

## Consequences

Anyone picking Tidewater up can read why it is the way it is. The records are history: the current rules are in
the numbered documents above this folder.
