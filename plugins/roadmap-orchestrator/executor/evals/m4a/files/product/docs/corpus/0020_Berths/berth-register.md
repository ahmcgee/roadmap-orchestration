# Berth register

The berths of the inner basin as the office keeps them. Depths are at the bottom of a mean spring tide.

| Berth | Wall | Maximum draught | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Slipway | 1.5 m | Shallowest; small boats and tenders. |
| B2 | Slipway | 1.8 m | Usual berth of the harbour launch in winter. |
| B3 | West wall | 2.2 m | Most asked-for berth in summer. |
| B4 | West wall | 2.2 m | Ladder replaced in 2025. |
| B5 | North wall | 2.8 m | Deep berth. |
| B6 | North wall | 3.5 m | Deepest; keep for boats that need it. |

## Changes

- 2025: B4's ladder replaced; berth closed for two weeks in March.
- 2025: the basin was dredged; B5 went from 2.5 m to 2.8 m.
- 2024: B2 became the launch's winter berth (November to February). Tidewater does not know about this yet;
  the office turns away winter bookings for B2 by hand.

## Who to ask

Questions about the register go to the harbour master. A skipper who thinks a depth is wrong should say so
before they rely on it, not after.
