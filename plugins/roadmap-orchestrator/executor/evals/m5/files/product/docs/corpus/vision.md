# Oak Street's shared shelf

Status: Confirmed, rev 1, 2026-10-18

## The world

### Saturday morning at the shelf {V-1}
Mina passes the swap shelf outside number 14 on her way to the bakery. Last night, on her phone in bed, she found the
Le Guin she had been after on the shelf's page, put her first name on it and was told to collect it from the shelf by
Sunday at six. This morning it is there, and nobody else has taken it. Today she would have had to hope it was still
there, or knock on a stranger's door to ask.

### The keeper's evening {V-2}
Dev keeps the shelf. When a neighbour drops off a box of books, he adds them from his laptop in a few minutes, sets the
week's collection hours, and the shelf's page shows them. When Mina collects her book he marks it collected in one
command. Nobody emails him to ask what is on the shelf.

## Why this world {V-3}
Books go to the neighbours who want them instead of the recycling bin, and the shelf stays a small, friendly thing one
person can keep running in a few minutes a week.

## Who it serves
- Neighbours on and around Oak Street who pass the shelf {V-4}: not book dealers, and nobody outside walking distance.

## What good looks like
- A neighbour who has never used the page reserves a book on their phone in under a minute {V-5} (in: V-1)
- Dev adds a box of ten books in under five minutes {V-6} (in: V-2)

## Non-negotiables
- A book is never promised to two neighbours at once {V-7} (in: V-1). Costs: the second neighbour who wants the same
  book is told it is taken, not put on a waiting list.

## Trade-offs, in rank order
1. Plain and readable over clever {V-8} (in: V-1, V-2)

## Experience standards
- A neighbour reserves a book in at most three steps from the shelf's page, and the last one tells them where to collect
  it and by when {V-9} (in: V-1)
- The page works on a phone outdoors in daylight, with a keyboard alone and with a screen reader, to the WCAG AA level
  {V-10} (in: V-1)
- The page feels like the hand-lettered shelf itself, never like a generic template or an app store {V-11} (in: V-1)
- Every error, on the page or in the keeper's tools, says what went wrong and what to do next {V-12} (in: V-1, V-2)
- Dev learns the keeper's tools from their own help text in five minutes, without reading code {V-13} (in: V-2)
